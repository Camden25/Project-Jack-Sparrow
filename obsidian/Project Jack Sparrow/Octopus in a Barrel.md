Literally just an octopus in a barrel

**Primary Fire:** Shotgun
**Secondary Fire:** Grappling hook

**Ability 1:** Drop Anchor

**Story:** This is an octopus that once got washed up onto the captain’s ship and stole a barrel  from the deck  and then used his tentacle to grapple up to the crow's nest. But the captain’s parrot sat on his head and they became friends so now the octopus just hangs around on the ship. Also the octopus is missing 1 tentacle and no one knows why. Also it has hammerspace for the anchor it drops and everyone always questions it.
